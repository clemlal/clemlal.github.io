Asymptotic Statistics, M2 RI

Self-assessment

Questions on the whole class, from the modes of convergence to M and Z estimators, in the order of the lectures. The sections titled Exercises apply the same techniques to examples that were not treated in class. A question can have several correct answers, possibly all of them: tick every correct one, then check. If some boxes are wrong, you are told how many; try again, or show the solution, which explains every answer.

Questions tagged Pen and paper need a computation: do it on paper before ticking. Questions tagged Going further go beyond the class, to related results worth knowing. Your answers are saved in this browser.
